Welcome

This page provides English information for international participants interested in the 31st Annual Meeting of the Japanese Society of Physical Therapy Fundamentals.

The meeting will be held in Nagoya, Japan, under the theme “Future-oriented Fundamentals of Physical Therapy.” The official Japanese website remains the primary source for detailed and frequently updated information.

Update status: According to the official website, the abstract submission period was closed on May 22, 2026. This English subpage is intended as a stable reference page for overseas visitors and may not be updated frequently.

Call for Abstracts

Important notice for English abstracts: English abstracts are not accepted through the online submission system. Authors who wish to submit in English should use the designated format and submit it by email according to the official instructions.

Submission Period

April 1, 2026, 12:00 PM - May 22, 2026, 5:30 PM (JST)

  • No submissions or modifications will be accepted after the deadline.
  • Abstracts that do not comply with the guidelines or lack required ethical information may be rejected.

Expected Number of Abstracts

General Presentations (Poster): approximately 300 abstracts.

English Abstract Submission

  1. Download the official English abstract format from the official website.
  2. Fill out the form according to the instructions.
  3. Submit the completed file by email with the subject line: Abstract Submission_[Your Name].

Open the official English abstract submission instructions

Submission Guidelines

Number of submissionsEach presenting author may submit only one abstract per category. The same or similar abstract cannot be submitted to multiple categories.
AuthorsUp to 30 authors, including the presenting author and co-authors.
AffiliationsUp to 15 institutions.
LengthEnglish submissions: title up to 20 words; body up to 300 words.
Structure[Introduction/Purpose], [Methods], [Results], [Discussion], [Conclusion], [Ethical Considerations]
NotesPlease avoid platform-dependent characters.

Categories

  1. Experimental Medicine (Structure, Function, Information Science)
  2. Kinesiology
  3. Neurophysiology, including Motor Control and Motor Learning
  4. Exercise Physiology
  5. Physical Evaluation
  6. Science and Engineering
  7. Translational Research - from Laboratory Science to Clinical Practice

Registration Fee

Abstract submission is free of charge. However, some individuals may be required to pay an abstract registration fee depending on their professional license and membership status. Please confirm details on the official website.

Language Support

  • An English session will be arranged for poster presentations.
  • For Special Lectures and Educational Lectures, English or Chinese translations will be displayed on the main screen.
  • For Hands-on sessions, interpretation services may be provided depending on the session.
